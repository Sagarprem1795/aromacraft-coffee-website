V60 Pour-Over Method

Master the technique for clean, sparkling clarity and aromatic cup extraction.

Brewing Ratio & Parameters

Dose: 20g coffee | Water: 320g water (1:16 ratio) | Temp: 93°C (200°F) | Time: 3:15 min

  1. Rinse Filter and Preheat

    Place paper filter inside your V60 cone. Flush thoroughly with boiling water to remove paper taste and preheat server. Discard water.

  2. Add Ground Coffee & Bloom

    Add 20g medium-fine ground coffee. Pour 60g water in concentric circles. Wait 45 seconds to let carbon dioxide escape ("the bloom").

  3. Continuous Concentric Pours

    Pour slowly in smooth spirals until the scale reads 200g. At 1:45 min, pour remaining water up to 320g, keeping the kettle spout close to the slurry.

  4. Drawdown & Serve

    Give the brewer a gentle single swirl. Allow all water to draw down cleanly. Total time should land between 3:00 and 3:30 minutes. Swirl and serve!
