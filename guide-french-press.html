French Press Immersion Guide

Simple immersion brewing for heavy body and deep aromatics.

Parameters

Coffee: 30g coarse grind | Water: 500g boiling (95°C) | Time: 4:00 min

  1. Preheat the Carafe

    Fill the glass pot with hot water for 30 seconds, then discard it.

  2. Add Coffee and Saturate

    Add 30g coarse coffee. Pour all 500g hot water vigorously to submerge grounds.

  3. Rest & Crust Break

    Place lid on top (do not plunge). At 4 minutes, stir the floating crust 3 times with a spoon.

  4. Press & Pour

    Insert mesh filter, press gently downward, and immediately pour coffee into your mug.
