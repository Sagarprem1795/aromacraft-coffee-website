Single Origin Micro-Lot

Ethiopian Yirgacheffe

$19.50 / 340g (12oz)

Grown at an altitude of 2,100 meters in the Gedeo zone, this washed lot highlights intense florality, bright lemon zest acidity, and delicate black tea mouthfeel.

Roast Specifications

  • Origin: Yirgacheffe, Gedeo, Ethiopia
  • Processing: Fully Washed, Raised Bed Sun-Dried
  • Elevation: 1,950m – 2,200m
  • Roast Level: Light-Medium (Agtron 64/78)
