Farmer Equity

Fair Living Compensation

We bypass speculative commodity brokers and contract directly with farm washing stations. By guaranteeing multi-year contracts at pre-negotiated premiums, farming cooperatives can invest in clean water, education, and fair agricultural wages.

Agroforestry

Shade Canopy Agriculture

Sun-scorched clearings degrade nutrient richness. Every coffee shrub in our portfolio grows beneath native shade tree canopies, providing vital habitats for migratory birds, conserving soil moisture, and eliminating artificial fertilizers.

Circular Lifecycle

Zero-Waste Packaging

From roastery shipping mailers to in-café takeaway cups, every packaging asset is 100% commercially compostable. Our bags are crafted using renewable wood pulp kraft papers and lined with natural plant-derived PLA.
