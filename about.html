Our Story & Craft

From micro-farms across the coffee belt directly to your morning cup.

Rooted in Precision Roasting

Founded in 2021, AromaCraft began with a single vintage 5kg drum roaster and a dedication to highlighting origin terroir over dark, heavy roasts.

Every batch is roasted using profile curves tuned to the ambient moisture, bean density, and harvest season of the lot.

Our Three Principles

100% Traceability

We work directly with smallholder farmers and cooperatives, knowing the exact farm altitude, processing mill, and lot harvest date.

Fair Farm Margins

We pay an average of 40% above fair-trade floor minimums to support farm workers and soil regenerative investments.

Small Batch Freshness

Beans are roasted to order twice weekly, rested for degassing, and shipped in nitrogen-flushed compostable bags.
